ABOUT THE LABORATORY

Built for fabricators, foundries and manufacturers who can't afford a wrong number.

Rawnat Testing LLP (RTL) is a NABL accredited metal testing laboratory based in Faridabad, set up to give local industry — fabrication shops, foundries, forging units and manufacturers — direct access to lab-grade material verification without routing samples out of the region.

The lab covers the four tests that decide whether a batch of metal is fit for purpose: chemical composition to confirm grade, metallurgical examination to check microstructure and process history, hardness testing on digital and Brinell testers, and universal testing for tensile strength, bend and elongation.

Every sample is logged, tested against the referenced standard, and reported with the method, equipment and reviewer on record — so the certificate holds up wherever it's presented.

TESTING CAPABILITIES

Four test disciplines, one report.

Each service runs on calibrated equipment against the standard specified by your client, drawing or purchase order.

01 / CHEM

Chemical Composition Analysis

Elemental analysis of ferrous and non-ferrous metals to confirm grade and detect deviation before it becomes a production problem.

IS 228ASTM E415Spectro-analysis
02 / META

Metallurgical Testing

Microstructure examination, grain size and inclusion assessment to verify heat treatment, casting and process quality.

ASTM E3IS 7739Grain size
03 / HARD

Hardness Testing

Surface and bulk hardness on digital hardness testers and Brinell hardness testers, reported in HRC, HV or HBW as required.

ASTM E18ASTM E10IS 1500
04 / UTM

Universal Testing (UTM)

Tensile strength, yield point, bend and percentage elongation on a universal testing machine, per applicable material standard.

ASTM E8IS 1608Bend / Elongation
